AILIN PEREZ

UFC VEGAS 121 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

They call her Fiona.

Princess at the weigh-in. Ogre when the cage door shuts.

That's not a marketing bit Ailin Perez invented for the cameras. That's who Argentina sent.

She grew up watching her brothers play rugby and decided she needed a contact sport of her own. Twelve years old. Kung Fu. Sanda. Dad believed in her before anybody else did — she still names him as the hero. Red belt. Purple belt in BJJ. Two-time South American Sanda champ. Two-time South American MMA bantamweight champ. Samurai Fight House. Then the UFC.

Her first night in the Octagon? Submitted.

Most fighters shrink after that.

She went six straight instead.

Longest active win streak in women's bantamweight. Second-longest among every active woman on the roster behind Natalia Silva. Five of those six by decision — not because she's boring. Because she takes the fight where she wants it and keeps it there.

Here's the number American timelines barely talk about:

28 takedowns in UFC women's bantamweight.

Amanda Nunes sits at 32.

Four away from the Lioness.

She already owns the single-fight record in the division — 10 takedowns in one night against Ashlee Evans-Smith. She absorbs 1.58 significant strikes per minute at 135, best rate in women's bantamweight history. You don't hit her clean. You survive her top game and hope the judges remember you were vertical sometimes.

Chaos with a grin. Signature twerk at the weigh ins and after the win. Turned a Daniel Cormier cage interview into an awkward wait while she finished the celebration; DC blushing on camera, translator scrambling, Octagon looking sideways. Somehow somebody always ends up holding her for the moment.

She says Norma has "no code." Already claiming the fight inside Norma's head. Wants the finish loud enough to dance into title talks — Kayla Harrison, Amanda Nunes — and become the first Argentine champion this division has ever seen.

Saturday she gets ranked No. 2 Norma Dumont in the co-main at the Apex.

Ailin is ranked No. 4.

Not a tune-up. A ranked Brazilian who hates her guts… and a chance to keep climbing toward 32.

Princess on the scale.

Ogre when it counts.
