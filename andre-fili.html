ANDRE FILI

UFC VEGAS 122 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Share on XShare on Facebook

Andre Fili was 7 years old the first time he watched his dad beat his mom.

His mom raised the kids on her own. His dad was in and out of jail, and every time he got out, he showed up. Every time he showed up, it got violent. "I'd take my little sister and hide her to try to keep her safe," Fili said. A 7-year-old boy, hiding his little sister so she didn't have to see it.

"I couldn't do anything and I was so ashamed," he said. "I was always kind of ashamed that I didn't step up and stop my dad, but I was a little kid… there was nothing I could do." The last time his dad was around, he was 12. After that, more jail, prison.

Fili grew into trouble of his own. An angry teen, eager to fight. It caught up with him, and he wound up on probation. Eventually he met Urijah Faber after losing a regional title fight to one of Team Alpha Male's guys. Post-fight, blown-out knee, he found Urijah backstage and asked to come train with them. Faber would later say Fili has "a lot of friends who are dead. Friends who are in jail."

By then he was on house arrest, an ankle monitor strapped to his leg. House, gym, then back to the house. He had to go straight home after, or it would alarm. So he trained like that. Walked into Team Alpha Male with it on.

"I would probably be in jail right now if it wasn't for Team Alpha Male… that mother fucker saved my life."

Now he's 36.

Second-most featherweight appearances in UFC history.

Married to Melissa. They have a son, Valor. The kid who hid his sister from his dad is a dad now. "I'm a superhero to my son," he said. "I've broken the cycle, and I have changed the course of my family's history."

Last year he posted some young highlights of himself with the caption: "If you see this kid, tell him I'm proud of him."

Saturday he walks out against Kai Kamaka III on the main card at the Meta Apex, for his 28th UFC fight.

If you see that kid, tell him he made it.
