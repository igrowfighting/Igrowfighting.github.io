DUMPLING

POWER SLAP · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Before Dumpling got Dana White into slap, somebody had to get him into it.

He went to a mall for an autograph.

March 2019, Krasnoyarsk. Before he was Dumpling, Vasilii Kamotskii showed up to meet a bench press record holder and walked into a slap contest he'd never heard of. His friends pushed him. Three fights later he was the winner. 30,000 rubles and a nickname to take home from his favorite food, pelmeni: Dumpling.

Home is a farm. Two thousand hectares with his brothers. As a kid he boxed in the same class as Petr Yan. One of them became a UFC champ. The other became a Power Slap champ. Their old coach still buys milk from the farm.

Back then he told The Guardian slap was "a show, not a sport." He doesn't say that anymore. Now he treats it like a craft. He told Match TV about training with a towel in his teeth, the kettlebell hanging off it to build his neck. Feet planted. Everything generated from the hips and core. "Everything was deliberate."

When Power Slap called, he said no. He already had offers in Russia. They had to sweeten the deal. Dana told Tucker Carlson that Dumpling wanted $25,000 and three goats. He got both. Then he signed.

UFC's Alexander Volkov barely knew him before the debut. Still, days before the June 28, 2024 card, Volkov posted that he'd be in Dumpling's corner. On fight night he walked him out and pulled out a comb to fix his beard. He's been back in the corner since, including Power Slap 19.

This week in Abu Dhabi, Volkov stepped to the UFC 333 press conference mic and asked Dana how to get a title shot. Dana's relationship with the guy Volkov has been cornering is different. He told talksport, "He is absolutely the guy that got me into it," talking about Dumpling and Power Slap.

Today at Power Slap 25 in Abu Dhabi, Dumpling gets the rematch with champion Dayne "Da Hawaiian Hitman" Viernes to get the belt back.

He's still a farmer. The farm will be there when he gets home again.

But for now, cue the trademark:

Time to eat, Dumpling. Nom. Nom. Nom.
