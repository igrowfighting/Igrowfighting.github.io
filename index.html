Merab vs. Yan

I was locked in on this one. Calling it round by round. Then the decision came and I was yelling at my TV again.

By JK47 Official result Yan · 48–47 ×3

  • The Decision

Scores are JK47's, called live as the fight happened. The judges saw a different fight — all three of them.

Merab walks out first under the Georgia flag, papakha on, arms out, smiling. He taps hands, slaps himself awake, jogs in pumping a fist. The lights go dark, the arena turns to fireflies, then blue with a red aura. Yan comes next with both his sons, Russian flag tied on, checking back so the youngest doesn’t get lost. Kisses the cross, Hail Mary, kiss on the cheek, hug for the other one. One handed cartwheel into the cage. No wasted energy from either of them.

Tale of the tape: Merab 35 and 5'6" with a 68" reach, Yan 33 and 5'7½" with 67". Yan the favorite. They touch gloves. Merab gives the thumbs-up like he wasn’t sure Yan would.

Round One 10–9 MerabJK47's card

Round one is all Merab. Calf kick almost drops Yan. Overhand flush on the chin. Constant bounce, feints, body lock, head kick attempt, another takedown. Yan gets an underhook and a clean left cross, but Merab’s on the waist throwing till the bell. Yan’s known for starting slow. Saw it against Sandhagen. Volume heavy, damage not quite a 10-8. 10-9 Merab.

Round Two 10–9 Merab2–0

Round two he keeps it up. Head kick into a jab before the foot even lands on the floor by Merab. Back take so fast I don’t know what to call it. Slams, ankle grabs, knees to the thigh. Yan lands a heavy two and a combo, but Merab’s diving on him like he’s going into a pool. Yan’s arms are getting heavy. Still hasn’t really gotten going. Small cut on Merab’s forehead. He’s still smiling. 10-9 Merab. 2-0. Round three’s gonna be important.

Round Three 10–9 Merab3–0

Round three Merab’s still constant. Clean right to the body early. Duck and grab the waist just to occupy. Separate, one two, right back in, hook the leg, grab both hips. Volume insane. Duck again, grab the hip, separate close and stay feinting. Heavy body kick from Yan gets a thud. Merab puts his arms out like come on. Clean overhand lands right on the chin. Yan’s hands drop, he’s shaking them out and takes a deep breath at 2:45. Merab circling the whole cage. Grabs a leg just to let go and circle out. Yan tries to exchange, Merab ducks, grabs the leg, circles, charges with a calf kick. Yan lands a heavy two. Merab answers. Wild sprawl across the octagon. Immediate grab again by Merab. Clean body kick from Yan, Merab acknowledges it. Yan closing, Merab runs away smiling. Another sprawl, almost gets the back. 25 seconds left, Merab circling and flexing. Yan shaking his arms. Body kick doesn’t wipe the smile. Clean jab at the bell. Yan has moments, but he can’t get time to start his own offense. Merab never stops for more than three seconds. I still give it to Merab. Volume and time occupation aren’t close. 3-0. Yan’s gonna have to get a finish.

Round Four 10–9 Merab4–0, close

Round four. Yan’s finding strikes because Merab can’t keep the same pace. Clean head kick at 46 seconds. Body shots Merab will feel tonight. Spinning on each other’s backs like a jungle gym. Merab’s breathing is picking up hard in the corner. Yan pours water in his trunks. Tough round to score. Yan’s shots felt sharper, but the volume is still Merab’s. I could see people giving it to Yan. I still have Merab 4-0 but 3-1 isn't a crime.

Round Five MerabOn JK47's card

Round five. Head nod. Merab throws twenty strikes in twelve seconds. Yan answers, lands a clean left, a jump knee, body shots. Merab’s bouncing off the cage, not wobbly, just tired. Yan’s arms are falling. Merab still coming forward. Clean right, eats two. Blade kick. Ten seconds left, heavy one two. Merab puts his arms out and taunts as the clock expires. I think Merab just won the championship.

Merab hops the cage and walks over to Hunter Campbell, shaking his hand. Dana and a guy in Saudi attire standing there. Yan hops the cage right over by Merab too. He’s not gonna let it look like he lost that fight yet. Yan’s out there pumping up the crowd. Replays show Merab grabbing hips, Yan landing clean on the chin, body kicks, Merab wincing and laughing. Beautiful boxing from Yan in slow motion. More beautiful was Merab knowing it existed and making Yan react instead of create. Yan stands there with his two sons. Looks like his eldest knows it’s not gonna go their way.

The Decision

All three judges score it 48–47.
And still. Petr Yan wins.

I screamed at the TV. These are my exact words, my raw reaction:

“I don’t know what kind of f***ing robbery I just witnessed. If anything I would’ve given it four rounds to one. I can see three to two. How in the f*** did Merab just lose that fight? Absolute f***ing disgrace. This is the type of s*** that got me to quit watching boxing. Are you f***ing kidding me?”

— JK47, watching live
