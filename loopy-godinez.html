LOOPY GODINEZ

UFC VEGAS 122 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Share on XShare on Facebook

"We're going to Disneyland!" Loopy Godinez's dad told her in 2008 in Aguascalientes, Mexico. She was 14, the oldest of 4 daughters. She didn't buy it. She didn't want to go. Something felt off. Her family drove 5 hours to the Mexico City airport. They had only packed what they could cram fast.

Her dad, Carlos, ran a car lot in Mexico. Some dangerous people wanted protection money, and he didn't pay. So they shot up his cars. Then they called the house and listed his four daughters by name, and the schools they went to. Carlos had to keep his family safe.

At the airport, they called Vancouver over the speakers. "Where are we going? I thought you said we're going to Disneyland?" Loopy asked. "Change of plans," was her dad's reply. Loopy cried the whole flight. Her friends, her school, her routines, all just vanished.

The first place they lived in Canada was, in her words, "A shitty hotel. Everything was shitty!" They barely knew English but had moved to an English speaking city. Her dad painted hotel rooms to provide. Loopy cleaned the bar bathroom and collected cans. The two would also go door to door, offering to wash cars, acting it out with their hands because they didn't know English yet. As you can imagine, many doors were slammed in their faces. For a couple years it was a survival to just exist.

As the family slowly settled in, Loopy was able to go back to Judo, a craft she started at 9 years old. "The coaches would give me higher belts… because I was murdering everyone, pretty much." Then a knee injury moved her to jiu jitsu. Eventually she watched one of Ronda Rousey's first fights. "If she did it, I can do it."

She went to her first MMA gym. It didn't go well. "I would just turn my back, run away, and curl in the corner of the wall." One coach didn't think she'd make it "not even in a street fight." She started out 2-4-1 in her amateur fights. But she never gave up, she kept showing up.

By 2020 she had won the LFA belt.

The UFC called on short notice in 2021. She lost a split decision but never stopped showing up. 15 UFC fights. 3 were within 42 days, including two fights just 7 days apart, the fastest turnaround in modern UFC history. The first woman to win 4 UFC fights in one year. "I've never said no to a fight. I've never pulled out."

In April, she got choked out in the 2nd round. Her first time being stopped. "I f***ed up.... sometimes we fall and you just get up, learn from that and keep going."

She's still showing up.

Saturday she's back at the Meta Apex. No. 11 at strawweight, against Ketlen Souza, a hard puncher coming off a 94 second KO. One takedown puts Loopy alone at 2nd place in all time takedowns in the division.

And after all these years Loopy has an amazing outlook: "Not everybody gets to be in the UFC," she said this week.

Loopy has never been to Disneyland. She jokes that her dad still owes her the trip. But even without the Disney trip, she's been on one hell of a ride.

And she just keeps showing up, still.
