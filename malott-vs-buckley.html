MALOTT VS. BUCKLEY

UFC EDMONTON · ROUND BY ROUND

By JK47

I wasn't calm for this one. I was yelling at my TV, and that's not AI when I say that.

Mike Malott walks out first. Ranked 13, born in Cleveland, fighting in front of Canada. The cutmen get the Vaseline on him and he stands there with his hands up like Superman. Honestly, he looks like Clark Kent. He raises his hands to the Canadian crowd. Somewhere back in a white room there's some guys and an orange 39 jersey.

Then the boos start. The lights go dark and the whole arena turns this eerie blue. The song hits and here comes Joaquin Buckley out of St. Louis. He has a shirt over his shoulder and throws it into the crowd. Zip-up jacket, pecs huge, team whispering in his ear. He walks within two inches of Malott and doesn't give him anything.

Tale of the tape: Buckley is 5'10" with a 76" reach. Malott is 6'1" with a 73" reach. Malott was the favorite.

Joe Martinez gets on the mic. "Live from Rogers Place. Edmonton, make some noise." Malott looks low energy and does a double kiss. Buckley just stares at him, pounds his chest, and points to the sky. In the middle of the cage Buckley won't touch gloves. It wasn't disrespect. He stamped his feet like a bull and that was it.

Goddard claps and round one starts. They trade kicks. When Buckley throws it comes by like a bullet. Malott throws a head kick at 2:21. Buckley taunts him at 1:53. Malott lands a jab at 1:01. I'm watching Buckley's left eye the whole time. There's a flurry with 20 seconds left, then a feinted spinning kick at the bell. I couldn't score that round.

Round two they clinch against the cage. Malott has double underhooks but Buckley doesn't seem to mind being there. He's digging to the body and putting his head on Malott's chin. It's like he's learning from Malott while they're fighting. He lands an inside elbow, then a sneaky right hand as they separate. Malott isn't moving his feet on the jab. Dare I say it, Mike Malott's arms are getting heavy.

And right as I say that, Buckley walks into a right hand.

Then I'm screaming at the TV. Malott! Malott! He just kicked him in the liver! Buckley drops like he's unconscious. Something flies out of his mouth. A body kick! Mike Malott just stopped Buckley with a body kick!

They show the replay from above, slowed down, and it wasn't a body kick at all. He went for the body and the knee caught Buckley right in the chin. The mouthpiece went flying the other way, defying physics. What a spectacular knockout.

They help Buckley onto a stool. There's a guy with a camera right in his face. I know it's their job and I'm not trying to be like that, but you're hurt, you don't know where you are, you just lost, and there's a camera right there. Malott walks over and Buckley, still seated, pulls him into a handshake and a lean-in hug. All respect. Malott fist bumps Buckley's whole team. Goddard has to push one of Malott's coaches out of the way just to raise his hand. With that black curly hair and that huge smile while Paul Felder talks to him, he looks like Clark Kent again.

I'm going to end it there. He can tell his own story for the rest of the night. He earned it.
