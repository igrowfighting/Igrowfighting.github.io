NIKO PRICE

UFC VEGAS 122 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Niko Price already retired once this year. Nobody noticed.

Back in March, he fought Michael Chiesa in Seattle on 7 days' notice. It was supposed to be his last fight. Problem was, it was Chiesa's last fight too, and Chiesa got the whole send-off.

Price laid his gloves on the canvas. No mic. No interview. Then they grabbed his gloves and took a picture with Chiesa.

"I had to stand there and watch his whole thing… 'Why am I not getting mic time?'" Price said. "They took a picture of him with my gloves. I was like, 'Nooo, I wanted to do that picture with my gloves.'"

That's a hell of a way to go out after a decade in the UFC.

He wasn't retiring for himself, either. "I'm getting old, man. Everything hurts. I want to spend time with my kids and stop being so selfish." His wife told him it was getting really hard on their daughters.

Price has seven kids. "My wife is an angel… a nice, quiet lady that got thrown into the blender with me and now it's a house full of Thundercats."

This is a Cape Coral, Florida kid who got beat up by his brothers and cousins growing up. He wanted to be a cop, took an MMA class to get ready for the academy, and took a punch. "Wow, this is awesome. I love this."

Niko never fought safe. Spinning strikes, wild kicks, swinging from anywhere, finishing guys from his back. Fan friendly before anybody called it that. He came into the UFC unbeaten. He knocked out Randy Brown with hammerfists from his back. He knocked out James Vick with an upkick in Tampa, with his family in the crowd. Four Performance of the Night bonuses. Thirteen finishes in his career.

Months after Seattle, the UFC called him back...

"They were like, 'Would you like a proper retirement?' And I was like, 'Heck yeah.'"

He'd told himself the only way he'd fight again was if the UFC came to him and offered it. "That's exactly what they said, word for word, and to me, that was God speaking."

One last strike nobody saw coming. And we're all happy it happened.

Saturday he faces Leon Shahbazyan on the prelims at the Meta Apex for his 22nd and final UFC fight. Nearly 10 years after his debut.

And this time, the gloves and the picture are his.
