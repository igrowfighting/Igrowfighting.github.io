PEREZ VS. DUMONT

UFC VEGAS 121 · ROUND BY ROUND

By JK47

Before the fight even started, Ailin Perez was already telling you who she was.

She paced. Not eager. Prepared. Chest out, that strong stance, then it switched on a dime. Feminine strut to the center. Taunting twirl straight into Norma Dumont’s eyes. Hands together. Bow.

Cut to Dumont. Cocky smile. IMMORTAL on the mouthpiece. Childhood in that grin. Muscles that would make a lot of men skip the arm wrestle. Waving the crowd in. Flex.

Round one, Perez is the one circling. Dumont plants like the floor belongs to her. A snapping right rips Dumont’s head and after that she has to respect the hands. Bruise under the left eye, like the top of Perez’s skull found the cheekbone coming out of the clinch. Then Dumont kicks to the body. Mistake. Perez snatches it like a spider that’s been waiting six days for a fly.

Paramount freezes and eats the last minute. When the picture comes back Perez is still on top and Dumont is hugging the body so the punches can’t get home. Corners: Dumont not phased. She just looks like she’s been in a fight.

Round two Norma’s adjusting her trunks from the ground work as corners exit. Perez bouncing. More pep. She walks Dumont to the cage and the toss doesn’t stick. Dumont answers immediately. Same throw, out of nowhere, and Perez is on her back looking up at yellow and black.

Perez hugs her so she can’t posture. Lets go. Dumont comes down like a swarm. Perez scrambles out. She ate a couple. Nothing that stops her from getting vertical.

Back on the feet Dumont is a tree. Thick legs. Roots in the canvas. Almost no fluidity. Perez circling in the dark blue and white. Dumont’s shots keep her honest more than they hurt her. A side kick from Perez actually moves her. An angry calf kick when that right leg is lead. Head shake no when Perez goes fishing for a leg. A glimmer of hope for a tide shift. Norma has been treading water.

Then the grin. IMMORTAL on the mouthpiece after she slips a spinning back fist. That grin is ahead of the fight.

Late, Perez looks like she’s picking weapons. Standing in front of a cabinet choosing what to take down. Dumont feinting from five feet with no walk-forward. Talking. Plant and throw. Perez lands a clean one. Dumont laughs it off and smiles to the corner like the night is still hers.

Between rounds both look composed. Dumont blowing her nose into the towel. Perez wiping her mouth and face. That’s the whole scene.

Round three Perez comes out pumping the jab. Dumont catching them like mitts, then a snapping jab of her own. They trade. A clean right stumbles Dumont. She shakes her head no and walks back in like the stumble was a rumor.

This is Dumont’s best round and she still doesn’t follow. A four-punch flurry, a couple land, and she stays planted. Talking. Perez not looking at her face. Looking at the hips. Norma’s left foot glued like a basketball pivot after the pickup. Perez steps in, square to bladed, calf kicks finding the root. Then the cage. Knees to the body. Dumont smiling into her face. Perez doesn’t take the bait. Right hand like it was personal. Because it was.

The screen puts a number up. Dumont 15-13 significant strikes on the round. It does not look like the fight. Horn. Perez throws a heavy overhand left that lands anyway.

They stay in each other’s face. Perez double birds her. Ref in. Security pile like a fumbled football is live. Perez trots off and throws one more middle finger on the way to commercial.

Your card can argue round three. I had it Perez. Hardcore Dumont fans will take two and three. I don’t see Dumont winning that fight. All three judges 29-28 Perez. I say 30-27. She looks up like she wasn’t sure as her wrist is lifted. Arm up. One elegant step. Signature twerk. Dumont walked out yelling back into the cage. Not a brawl. Just too many bodies and a camera that can’t find the shot.

Felder asks. Perez goes to the condoms Dumont threw at her at the weigh-in. Says she couldn’t even use them. Translator stumbles. The room hears something vulgar and doesn’t get a clean English version. Then she pops off in Spanish like a gangster. Elegant and defiant in the center. Splits. Shakes her rump. Finally gives Dumont her flowers. Strength. She felt the punches. She was more technical. She won. It is what it is.

Ailin Perez might of just wiggled her way into a title shot.

And they say women’s MMA isn’t entertaining.
