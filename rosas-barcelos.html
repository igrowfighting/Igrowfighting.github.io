ROSAS JR VS BARCELOS

UFC VEGAS 121 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Share on XShare on Facebook

Rosas Jr doesn’t see an old guy. He sees an experienced fighter on a five-fight streak. He’s not making the trap of writing Barcelos off the way people write him off for being 21.

That respect hits harder when you clock the gap. 21 vs 39. Eighteen years. UFC's version of JK vs Blind Bear. First five-rounder of Rosas’s career. Barcelos turned pro in 2012 while Rosas was still in elementary school.

It’s #12 vs #13 at 135. Both on five-fight win streaks. First UFC main event for the kid. And he’s already talking about a two-year window to break Jon Jones’ record as the youngest UFC champion, short notice and all.

Books lean the prospect (-155). Tapology community still ~62% on the vet. One clean night from Barcelos and the coronation story dies Saturday.

Two weeks after the family ate that viral night, big brother headlines. The vet’s chance to kill the hype.

It’s #UFCVegas121. Saturday on Paramount+.
