SAME HANDS

POWER SLAP 25 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Same hands that held the newborn,
same hands that crossed the street with Grandma's arm.

Same hands that turned the soil, pulled the crop,
built the wall, held the baby through the storm.

The face behind it all,
it watched the seed go in,
it dreamed the harvest up
before the hands began.

And now one hand rises, alone.
One palm, one shot,
no second chance to block.

The brain that planned the field,
the eyes that saw it grow,
none of it matters
once the hand decides.
