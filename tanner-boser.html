TANNER BOSER

UFC EDMONTON · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Tanner Boser was supposed to make his UFC debut in Edmonton in 2019. His opponent tested positive the day before weigh-ins and the fight was scrapped.

"Now, seven years later, I get to fight for UFC in my home city."

Tonight "The Bulldozer" steps in against Brazil's Jhonata Diniz at Rogers Place. The UFC booked the matchup for a reason, Boser said: "They realized they can put me in there with guys like him, exciting guys, and they can have exciting heavyweight fights."

The road back wasn't clean. The UFC released him in 2023 after a win. He spent nearly three years outside the promotion, fighting at UAE Warriors and signing with the GFL before returning in April. Now 35, with 22 wins (12 by knockout), he still works a regular job at a maximum-security prison. Just a regular guy, chasing greatness like all our heroes.

On Tanner's bio, he's asked what he would use his UFC platform for. His response was beautiful. Benny. A young boy who used to train at his gym and beat cancer, but now it has returned. Tanner said he'd use his platform to help Benny's family raise money for his treatment in the USA. You can find Benny's GoFundMe in the replies below.

Benny is 12 and has been fighting cancer since he was 5. His family is raising money for his treatment in the USA, and every bit helps. Tanner's already in his corner. Let's join him. 🥊

On the fight itself, Boser pointed to the heavyweight division's recent shift: "It was an issue a couple of years ago, people were complaining that the heavyweights were boring. Well, it's in a resurgence right now. Matchups like this are what they want for the viewers. We'll see, somebody is going to sleep in this one."

Edmonton, it's your guy's night. Seven years in the making. Good luck, Bulldozer.
