UFC 332 ON CBS

UFC 332 · BEYOND THE ROUNDS

By JK47

Natalia Silva was supposed to fight Valentina Shevchenko for the belt. Then Val got hurt, vacated, and the whole division got rewritten in a week. Now it’s #1 Silva vs #8 Wang Cong for the vacant flyweight title for the main event UFC 332 in Salt Lake City.

And this one’s not hiding behind a streaming login.

For the first time, the entire UFC main card is live on CBS and Paramount+ at the same time. Not a two-hour tease. The whole thing. Free on network TV. The bar that won’t buy Paramount+ can still flip on Channel 2 and put a title fight on every screen in the building.

That’s bigger than it sounds. The ESPN years locked numbered cards behind pay-per-view. Streaming took over. A lot of places never put UFC on the wall again. Oct 3, the main card is back where anybody with a regular TV can find it.

Silva’s the one who’s been clearing the division the hard way. 14 fights without a loss. 8-0 in the UFC. Her last three: Rose Namajunas. Alexa Grasso. Jessica Andrade. Former champs, back to back to back. Silva's first UFC main event, and it’s the first full UFC main card America gets on CBS.

Wang Cong didn’t get here by waiting in line.

She KO’d Victoria Leonardo in 62 seconds on debut, the second-fastest women’s flyweight finish in UFC history. Lost once (submission to Fernandes), then ran four straight, including Tracy Cortez this summer. And before any of that MMA resume mattered, she already beat Valentina… in kickboxing, Kunlun Fight, Halloween 2015. Valentina’s last kickboxing fight. Wang’s still talking about it a decade later today.

That’s the real heat sitting under this card. Not Silva vs Wang interviews (those haven’t started yet). It’s Wang vs the ghost of the champ who just vacated.

She called Valentina “Box Office Poison.” Said she ducked a Macau Q&A stage with her. After Cortez, she went on IG and said she can “smash your mouth” — in America this time — and demanded the title shot.

She got the shot. Just not against Valentina. Rather against the #1 who’s been beating every former champ they throw at her.

Salt Lake. Vacant belt. Entire main card on CBS. One walks out the new flyweight queen while Valentina sits out a year and waits to take it back. And half the country can watch it without a subscription.
